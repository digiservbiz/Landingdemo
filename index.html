Frequently Asked

Questions Before You Start

How long does a typical renovation take?

A kitchen or bathroom typically takes 2–4 weeks, an extension 8–12 weeks, and a full house renovation 3–6 months. You'll get a specific, fixed timeline in your quote.

Do you handle permits and paperwork?

Yes — we prepare and file all required municipal permits for extensions and structural work as part of the design phase, before construction begins.

How is the quote priced — is it really fixed?

After the site visit and design phase, you receive one fixed-price quote covering labor and materials. It only changes if you request additional scope in writing.

Can we stay in the house during renovation?

For most single-room projects, yes. For full-house renovations we'll advise honestly during the consultation — sometimes staying is fine, sometimes it isn't worth the disruption.

What warranty do you offer?

Every project includes a 5-year workmanship warranty, on top of the standard 10-year structural insurance required by Belgian law for new construction.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Brussels and Walloon Brabant, including Uccle, Ixelles, Etterbeek, Woluwe and Waterloo. Get in touch if you're just outside this area.
